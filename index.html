A letter from your Nani

My dearest Alekhya,

I don't always know how to put my feelings into perfect words. Sometimes I am happy, sometimes I overthink, and sometimes I feel so much that I don't know where to begin. So I made this little website—to show you the feelings I struggle to explain.

I chose these pictures because each one carries a piece of our story. I added these reels because some feelings remind me of you immediately. And I wrote these words because I wanted you to see the effort behind this, not just the finished page.

As you go to the hostel tomorrow, I know I will miss you. But I want you to feel loved, not held back. Follow your dreams, take care of yourself, and remember that distance can make us miss each other, but honest communication and care can help us stay close.

Thank you for the smiles, the silly moments, the comfort and the memories we have made. I cannot promise every day will be easy, but I can promise to keep trying to understand you, respect you and make our moments count. If you ever wonder how much you mean to me, come back to this little cinema. I made it with you in my heart.

With all my love, your Nani ♡
